Privacy Policy – Voice Text

Applies to the Voice Text Android app and the Voice Text Keyboard by Seeways Apps. Last updated: 11 November 2026.

Voice Text turns voice notes into text, reads messages aloud, translates between Urdu, Roman Urdu and English, and offers a keyboard with the same tools. This policy explains what data the app uses and why.

What we process, and only when you ask

  • Voice notes you share to Voice Text (for example from WhatsApp using Share) – to turn them into text.
  • Text messages you share or copy – only when you tap Listen, Translate, اردو, Roman or English.
  • Text you type in the Voice Text Keyboard – only when you tap a translate button (→ English, → Roman, → اردو).
  • Your voice from the microphone – only while you hold the keyboard's 🎤 voice-typing session that you started yourself.

Normal typing on the keyboard is never sent anywhere. The app does not read your chats in the background and does not use Accessibility services.

Where the data goes

  • Voice notes, text and spoken words you choose to process are sent securely (HTTPS) through the Voice Text service (our small relay running on Cloudflare) to Google's Generative AI service (Gemini API), which creates the transcript, translation or audio. Google's terms and privacy policy apply to that processing.
  • The Voice Text service only passes your content on to Google and returns the result. It does not store or log your voice notes or messages, and we cannot see them.
  • To keep the service fair for everyone, it counts how many times each install uses it per day. For this it uses a random install ID created by the app (not linked to your name, phone number or account) and your internet (IP) address. Both are stored only as one-way scrambled codes (hashes) with the daily count, and old counts are deleted.
  • Voice typing on the keyboard uses your phone's Google speech recognition service.

What is stored on your phone

  • Your settings (output language, keyboard colour, size, font), the random install ID, and your last results ("Recent") are stored only on your device. You can clear Recent in the app at any time, or remove everything by uninstalling the app.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell personal data or share it for advertising.
  • We do not collect contacts, location, photos or files.
  • We do not use analytics or tracking in the current version.

Permissions

  • Microphone – only for keyboard voice typing, when you tap 🎤.
  • Internet – to send the item you chose to the AI service.

Advertising

The app includes Google AdMob so that it can show ads in a later version; the current version shows no ads. When ads are switched on, they appear only inside the app (never inside the keyboard), and AdMob may use your device's advertising ID and basic device information to show and measure ads, as described in Google's advertising policy. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's Settings → Privacy → Ads. Users in the EEA and UK will be asked for consent first.

AI results

Transcripts, translations and voices are created by AI and may contain mistakes. Please check important information. You can report a wrong or offensive result with the "Report" button, which opens an email to us.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13.

Not affiliated

Voice Text is an independent app and is not affiliated with, endorsed or sponsored by WhatsApp, Meta or Google.
